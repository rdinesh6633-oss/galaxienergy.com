Inside the Technology

Built Different.
Engineered Better.

Advanced power architecture that delivers real performance advantages over traditional systems.

01

Advanced Power Conversion Architecture

Dual Active Bridge (DAB) DC-DC topology provides galvanic isolation between battery and inverter. This is the gold standard in modern power electronics — not available in any traditional inverter.

02

~94.5% Inverter Efficiency

Traditional inverters run at 85–88%. That 6–9% difference is real money saved daily, compounding over 10 years. More energy reaches your home, less is wasted as heat.

03

DSP-Based Digital Control

Digital Signal Processor manages all power conversion with microsecond precision — faster, more accurate, and more stable than the analog control circuits inside traditional inverters.

04

Fast Hardware Protection

Hardware-level fault detection for over-current, short circuit, and thermal events. Reacts in microseconds — before software can respond. Your system is protected before you even notice.

05

LiFePO4 Battery Chemistry

Safest lithium technology. No thermal runaway. 3,000+ cycles at 80% DoD — 8 to 10 years of daily use under Indian conditions. Outlasts lead-acid by 3–4×.

FAQ

Quick Answers

Can I add battery modules later?
▾
Yes. Modules use blind-mate connectors and can be added anytime without rewiring. Stack the module and the system detects it automatically.
Can it be wall-mounted like a TV?
▾
Yes. Mounts cleanly like an LED TV. Zero floor space used. Premium aesthetic on any wall. Under an hour to install.
Can it stand on the floor too?
▾
Yes. Included support bush allows stable floor-standing. No drilling required. Keep it portable and move as needed.
Does it support solar panels?
▾
Yes. Built-in solar hybrid input. AI decides whether to use solar directly, charge battery, or draw from grid — based on your mode and real-time conditions.
What is AI Mode?
▾
AI Mode uses your usage patterns, weather forecast, power cut history, and location data to optimize every decision automatically — daily, with zero manual input from you.
Is the 2-year IoT plan free?
▾
Yes. Every unit includes 2 years of free premium IoT — monitoring, smart alerts, and AI optimization from day one. No subscription needed for 2 years.
Is battery included in the price?
▾
UPS unit and LiFePO4 battery modules are sold separately. Choose exactly the capacity you need and add modules over time as requirements grow.
Can it run an air conditioner?
▾
3kW model can run a 1-ton inverter-type AC for limited hours depending on battery. Non-inverter ACs and multi-ton units are not recommended.
